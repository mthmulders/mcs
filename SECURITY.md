# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

Only the latest released version is "supported", that is, on an best-effort basis.
There is no guarantee or commitment that an issue **will** be fixed, or a feature **will** be added.

I am developing MCS in my spare time.
Therefore, I may decide to spend that time on other things I consider important or valuable purely of my own accord.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

No policy yet, please just open an issue.
